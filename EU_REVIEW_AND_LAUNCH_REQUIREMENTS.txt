EU EDUCATION REVIEW AND LAUNCH REQUIREMENTS
Reviewed 17 November 2026 | Education only | Intended audience: all EU countries

FINDING
There is no single cookie banner, disclaimer or English-language page that
provides blanket EU-wide legal clearance. The public static pages were revised
conservatively. Actual course videos, member services, contract checkout,
advertising creatives and the operator's authorisation status were not audited.
Do not represent the result as an approved or universally compliant EU site.

CONTENT ACTIONS
Removed the account credit and preferred-broker/IB funnels rather than adding a
disclaimer to them. ESMA's CFD incentive Q&A addresses regulated CFD providers;
its exact national application is not inferred for every education business.
Removal here also follows the owner's express education-only scope. No claim
is made that every referral or education subscription is inherently unlawful.

Removed unverifiable expertise/service availability and aspirational financial
outcome claims. Kept general explanations, invented-price chart examples and
balanced risk information. No invented loss percentage, licence number, award,
testimonial, guaranteed outcome or regulatory endorsement was added.

Actual recommendations, signals or automated copying cannot be relabelled as
education. Have a qualified reviewer inspect each lesson, live-session format,
trainer practice, ad and any actual economic interest before reconnecting it.

PRIVACY
ePrivacy storage/access rules are implemented nationally. GDPR governs personal
data processing and consent standards. The EDPB taskforce findings provide a
minimum/common position, not a stand-alone approval or override of national law.
This build implements positive choices, first-layer rejection, granular options,
no optional defaults and an always-available withdrawal route. Local consent
records alone may not meet the operator's full accountability arrangements.

The controller must approve identity/contact information, lawful bases for each
processing purpose, processor/recipient information, hosting log retention,
rights/complaints routes and international-transfer information. Assess whether
an EU representative or other country-specific disclosure is needed. No Meta
or analytics service runs in the delivered build. Any future provider requires
specific disclosure, transfer/vendor assessment and a real consent/network test.

CONSUMER / CHECKOUT
Before offering paid education, confirm the actual contracting entity, service
scope, currency, total tax-inclusive price, payment/performance arrangements,
term, renewal, cancellation, complaints and withdrawal information. State an
unambiguous obligation to pay at the order step, and provide confirmation in a
durable medium. Services and digital content have different withdrawal rules;
do not use a blanket 'access means no refunds' or pre-ticked rights waiver.

Directive (EU) 2023/2673 introduced an online withdrawal-function requirement in
the Consumer Rights Directive framework. National measures apply from 19 June
2026. Assess its application to the actual online education contract and provide
the function, confirmation step and durable-medium acknowledgement where due.
An information page or a nonfunctional cancellation button does not implement
this. The package therefore has no paid checkout or purported withdrawal form.

COUNTRY / BUSINESS CHECKS STILL REQUIRED
Confirm each intended country's consumer-information languages, advertising and
financial-promotion boundaries, mandatory trader/imprint details, applicable
national cookie guidance, tax treatment, complaint/ADR requirements and digital
accessibility obligations/exemptions. Confirm all claims/portraits/teaching media
have permission and reflect the actual offering. English-only copy is not a
confirmation that all national language obligations have been met.

LAUNCH STATUS
17 owner-requested placeholders and 3 review flags remain open. The public build
is staged/noindex with paid access, external member portal and marketing off.
The host must password-protect private review. Completing configuration and
passing automated checks does not substitute for qualified national review.
